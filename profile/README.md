**One community. More ways to connect. Across your devices.**

CoolVibes is a multilingual **LGBTQIA+ social network, friendship and dating platform for adults aged 18+ and respectful allies**. Share your world, discover people nearby and worldwide, join conversations, explore places and build relationships in one connected community.

**Web & installable PWA · iPhone & iPad · Android · macOS · Windows · Linux · ChromeOS · VS Code**

[Open CoolVibes](https://coolvibes.lgbt) · [coolvibes.app](https://coolvibes.app) · [coolvibes.io](https://coolvibes.io) · [VS Code](https://github.com/CoolVibesLGBT/vscode_extension)

> **Türkçe:** CoolVibes; sosyal medya, arkadaşlık, tanışma, özel ve grup sohbetleri, yakınındaki kişiler, mekânlar ve topluluk deneyimini bir araya getirir. 18 yaş ve üzeri LGBTQIA+ bireyler ve topluluğa saygı duyan kişiler içindir. 23 dil/bölge seçeneği, kişiselleştirme ve profil görünürlük kontrolleriyle telefon, bilgisayar ve tarayıcı arasında bağlantıda kalın. Uyumlu tarayıcılarla PC, Mac, Linux, Chromebook, Android, iPhone ve iPad üzerinde uygulama olarak yükleyebilir; ana ekranınızdan, masaüstünüzden veya Dock üzerinden açabilirsiniz. Native iOS/Android uygulamaları ve ayrı Chrome eklentisi henüz yayımlanmadı; web/PWA üzerinden hemen katılabilirsiniz.

## More than a single way to meet

Your community life includes more than a match. CoolVibes brings **social publishing, friendship, dating, messaging, groups, places and live experiences** into the same product. Move from discovering a profile to a conversation, from a post to a shared interest, or from a nearby place to a new connection.

- **Express yourself:** profiles, interests, preferences, public and private photos, posts and media.
- **Choose how you connect:** followers, friendships, likes, matches, private messages and group conversations.
- **Explore beyond the feed:** nearby discovery, maps, places, check-ins, events and community listings.
- **Feel at home in your language:** 23 locale variants, regional illustrations and right-to-left layouts for Arabic, Persian and Hebrew.
- **Shape your experience:** visibility settings, private-photo access controls, blocking/reporting, themes and chat personalization.
- **Stay connected across devices:** browser access today, with mobile, desktop and extension experiences in the product ecosystem. Release details are listed below.

## Install it. Keep your community close.

Use CoolVibes on your **phone, tablet, laptop or desktop**. The installable web app (PWA) gives you an icon on your home screen, app launcher, desktop or Dock and an app-style window in supported environments. You can start using this experience while native mobile store releases are pending.

| Device | How to add CoolVibes |
| --- | --- |
| Windows, Linux, Chromebook | Open the website in a compatible browser and choose **Install** / **Install page as app**. |
| Mac | Use a compatible browser's installation menu, or Safari on macOS Sonoma 14+ → **File → Add to Dock**. |
| Android phone or tablet | Open in Chrome → browser menu → **Install**, then confirm. Menu wording varies. |
| iPhone or iPad | Open in Safari → **Share → Add to Home Screen**; keep **Open as Web App** enabled when offered. |

Installation depends on your browser and operating system. Notifications require permission and supported delivery; Safari/Opera currently use a fallback. An internet connection is required for feeds, messages and other connected activity. [Detailed device guide and browser references](https://github.com/CoolVibesLGBT/.github/blob/HEAD/llms-full.txt).

## A profile with room for the whole person

Introduce yourself with an **avatar, cover, rich biography, website, languages and optional personal details**. Share your personality through posts, photos and interests, then choose which audience can see protected information.

The source profile catalog includes **850 options across 50 categories and groups**:

- **32 personal-attribute categories · 510 options:** gender identity, orientation, role, physical characteristics, lifestyle, relationship intentions, partner preferences, children, pets, diet, education, personality, daily rhythm, humor, religion, optional health information and adult preferences.
- **13 interest groups · 274 options:** activities and shared interests that help people find common ground and begin conversations.
- **5 adult preference groups · 66 options:** optional self-expression for the adult community, subject to visibility and access rules.

Your profile connects to posts, replies, media, friends and photo collections. Followers, likes, matches and visits add engagement context where your account can access them. Travel-related information is represented in the product data scope; client editors and availability vary.

Choose **public, friends-only, followers-only, mutuals-only or private** profile visibility. Manage private-photo access through requests, approval/decline, owner grants and revocation. Following, friendship, matching and private-photo access each have their own meaning and controls.

[Explore every profile category and option](https://github.com/CoolVibesLGBT/.github/blob/HEAD/docs/profile-catalog.md) · [Read the complete product experience](https://github.com/CoolVibesLGBT/.github/blob/HEAD/llms-full.txt)

## Why choose CoolVibes?

**More ways to belong.** Social participation, friendship, dating and groups let you choose how a connection develops. Posts, interests and detailed profiles provide context before a conversation.

**More control over sharing.** Audience settings, private-photo permissions, friendship requests, blocking and reporting give you practical tools to manage participation and build trust at your own pace.

**Closer to your language and your devices.** 23 locale variants, regional artwork, RTL layouts and installable web access bring the community to supported phones, tablets and computers. Themes, chat patterns, gradients and wallpapers help you make the experience your own.

**More community beyond a profile.** Polls, event responses, places, check-ins, group conversations and classifieds add ways to participate around shared interests and local activity.

These are the concrete reasons to consider CoolVibes. A universal competitor ranking or guaranteed relationship outcome is not established by this profile.
