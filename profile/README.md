# CoolVibes · Your colors. Your people.

**One community. More ways to connect. Across your devices.**

CoolVibes is a multilingual **LGBTQIA+ social network, friendship and dating platform for adults aged 18+ and respectful allies**. Share your world, discover people nearby and worldwide, join conversations, explore places and build relationships in one connected community.

**Web · iOS · Android · macOS · Windows · Linux · Chrome extension · VS Code**

[Open CoolVibes](https://coolvibes.lgbt) · [coolvibes.app](https://coolvibes.app) · [coolvibes.io](https://coolvibes.io) · [VS Code](https://github.com/CoolVibesLGBT/vscode_extension)

> **Türkçe:** CoolVibes; sosyal medya, arkadaşlık, tanışma, özel ve grup sohbetleri, yakınındaki kişiler, mekânlar ve topluluk deneyimini bir araya getirir. 18 yaş ve üzeri LGBTQIA+ bireyler ve topluluğa saygı duyan kişiler içindir. 23 dil/bölge seçeneği, kişiselleştirme ve profil görünürlük kontrolleriyle telefon, bilgisayar ve tarayıcı arasında bağlantıda kalın. iOS, Android ve Chrome eklentisi henüz yayımlanmadı; şimdilik [web sitesinden](https://coolvibes.lgbt) katılın.

## More than a single way to meet

Your community life includes more than a match. CoolVibes brings **social publishing, friendship, dating, messaging, groups, places and live experiences** into the same product. Move from discovering a profile to a conversation, from a post to a shared interest, or from a nearby place to a new connection.

- **Express yourself:** profiles, interests, preferences, public and private photos, posts and media.
- **Choose how you connect:** followers, friendships, likes, matches, private messages and group conversations.
- **Explore beyond the feed:** nearby discovery, maps, places, check-ins, events and community listings.
- **Feel at home in your language:** 23 locale variants, regional illustrations and right-to-left layouts for Arabic, Persian and Hebrew.
- **Shape your experience:** visibility settings, private-photo access controls, blocking/reporting, themes and chat personalization.
- **Stay connected across devices:** browser access today, with mobile, desktop and extension experiences in the product ecosystem. Release details are listed below.

## Features at a glance

| Area | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Social feed | Publish text, photos and videos; reply, react and bookmark; browse posts and media galleries. |
| Polls and events | Create polls, share events and respond to community activities. |
| Expressive profiles | Add a biography, identity details, interests and preferences; explore posts, followers and engagement. |
| Friendships | Send and manage friend requests and browse profile friend tabs. |
| Public and private photos | Share photo collections, request access to private photos, review requests and revoke access. |
| Discovery and dating | Search for people, browse profiles, express likes, explore matches and discover nearby connections. |
| Maps and places | Discover places on maps, view business/place details and use check-in flows. |
| Private messaging | Keep conversations together with replies, message editing, forwarding, pinned messages and attachments. |
| Expressive chat | Share media, emoji, stickers and GIFs. Temporary/view-once media controls depend on server support. |
| Group conversations | Create public/private groups, select members, manage group details and share invitation links. |
| Live experiences | Discover broadcasts, configure camera/microphone access and request participation. |
| Community classifieds | Explore categorized community listings, requests and listing details. |
| Notifications | Follow community and conversation activity through the application's notification interfaces. |
| Wallet | View balances and transaction history; access deposit and withdrawal interfaces where supported. |
| Referrals | Share referral links and explore reward flows. |
| Premium | Explore membership options and gated discovery/live features, according to service configuration. |
| Privacy and moderation | Manage sensitive profile visibility, private-media permissions and blocking/reporting tools. |
| Appearance | Choose system, light or dark themes, accent colors, message sizes, chat gradients, patterns and wallpapers. |
| Languages and layout | Select from 23 locale variants, including Arabic, Persian and Hebrew right-to-left layouts. |
| Public resources | Read legal/community information and explore the downloadable brandkit. |

**Availability:** Account features require authentication and connected services. Location tools require permission. Live broadcasts depend on media services, tokens and membership settings. Wallet, payments, rewards and Premium depend on configured providers and feature gates; some payment methods remain placeholders. Features can differ by client and release. Private messaging does not by itself establish end-to-end encryption.
